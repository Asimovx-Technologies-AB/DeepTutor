# 04 — Validation and Marketing Plan

## Objective

The first marketing activity is customer discovery. The goal is to learn whether Swedish parents experience a sufficiently painful preparation problem and will pay for a managed solution.

## Phase 1 — Competitor benchmark

Use the same real Swedish grades 7–9 Biology material in the current product and major alternatives.

### Test set

- Teacher assessment instructions
- Textbook or handout pages
- Presentation
- Study questions
- A known misconception
- A diagnostic and final assessment set

### Compare

- Scope identification
- Swedish terminology
- Grounding and citations
- Diagnostic usefulness
- Plan quality
- Explanation and hint quality
- Misconception handling
- Parent visibility
- Safety
- Latency
- Cost
- Student and parent preference

## Phase 2 — Thirty parent discovery conversations

Conduct interviews before presenting the application.

### Core questions

- How does your child prepare for a Biology or theory-heavy test today?
- How do you know what will be tested?
- How do you know what the child does not understand?
- What happens when you cannot help with the subject or Swedish terminology?
- Which products, tutors, or school resources do you use?
- What works and what is missing?
- Would you upload school material to a protected AI service?
- Would a concise readiness and improvement report be useful?
- What would make you distrust such a service?
- What have you paid for learning support previously?
- Would you pay 99–199 SEK for one managed preparation period?

Do not lead parents toward the desired answer.

## Phase 3 — Five-family concierge pilot

Deliver the proposed service with manual review before automating the complete workflow.

For every family:

1. Collect consent, test date, and school material.
2. Review extraction and curriculum mapping manually.
3. Deliver the diagnostic.
4. Review the generated gap map and plan.
5. Monitor session completion.
6. Review generated assessment content.
7. Deliver the final report.
8. Interview parent and child separately.
9. Ask the family to pay for the next preparation period.

The strongest validation signal is a second purchase, not positive feedback.

## Phase 4 — Twenty-family product pilot

Use the product workflow with limited manual support. Recruit families who have a real assessment within two to four weeks.

### Pilot offer

> Four-week Swedish Biology Test Preparation Pilot for school years 7–9. Includes a diagnostic, personal daily plan, guided AI learning, final practice assessment, and parent improvement report.

Show the intended regular price from the beginning.

## Pricing tests

### Test Preparation Pass

- 14–30 days
- One assessment goal
- One child
- Diagnostic, plan, tutoring, practice, and report
- Price test: 99, 149, or 199 SEK

### Ongoing plan

- Continued preparation periods and longer mastery history
- Price test only after repeat demand is observed
- Initial hypothesis: 149–249 SEK per month

Prices are validation hypotheses, not commitments.

## Positioning and messaging

### Primary message

> Stop guessing whether your child is prepared for the test.

### Explanation

> Upload the material from school. We identify the gaps, create a daily plan, guide the preparation, and show what improved.

### Multilingual support message

> Correct Swedish school terminology, with explanations the family can understand.

## Initial acquisition channels

1. Founder and AsimovX network.
2. Malmö and Skåne parent communities.
3. Indian and other multilingual family communities in Sweden.
4. Local tutoring and homework-support providers.
5. Paid Swedish Biology teachers as advisors and pilot reviewers.
6. Parent referrals from completed pilots.

Teachers should be recruited as advisors, not pressured to market a commercial product to their students.

## Landing page

Required sections:

1. Parent problem and product promise.
2. Who the pilot is for.
3. How the preparation journey works.
4. Example diagnostic gap map.
5. Example daily plan.
6. Example parent improvement report.
7. Safety and privacy summary.
8. Founder and AsimovX identity.
9. Pilot price or transparent pricing test.
10. Waitlist/application form.

### Waitlist fields

- Parent contact details
- Child's school year
- Upcoming subject and approximate test date
- Main preparation difficulty
- Preferred explanation/report language
- Existing learning support used
- Interest in feedback participation

Do not collect unnecessary child data during lead capture.

## Content plan

Content should teach parents about preparation problems rather than advertise AI features.

### Themes

- Why rereading is not the same as learning.
- How to discover what a child actually understands.
- Active recall and delayed revision.
- Helping without giving the answer.
- Swedish subject terminology in multilingual homes.
- How to make a realistic plan before a test.
- Anonymous pilot examples and measured improvements.
- Product limitations, safety, and responsible AI use.

### Formats

- Short Facebook and Instagram posts
- Founder-led LinkedIn updates
- Brief explanatory videos
- Parent readiness checklist
- Online parent information session
- Pilot case studies after consent

## Paid acquisition

Do not run significant paid advertising before the concierge pilot shows repeat willingness to pay.

After validation, test a limited 3,000–5,000 SEK campaign focused on Malmö, Lund, Helsingborg, Gothenburg, and Stockholm. Optimise for completed diagnostics and paid preparation periods—not clicks.

## Funnel metrics

| Stage | Initial target |
|---|---:|
| Relevant leads | 100 |
| Parent interviews | 30 |
| Concierge pilot families | 5 |
| Product pilot families | 20 |
| Diagnostic completions | 15+ |
| Four-week active learners | 10+ |
| Initial paying families | 5–8 |
| Repeat purchases | 3+ |

## Trust assets

- Identifiable Swedish company and responsible contact.
- Teacher/advisor involvement.
- Plain-language child privacy information.
- Explanation of how uploaded material is handled.
- Explanation of AI limitations and answer-reporting.
- Example learning evidence and parent report.
- Transparent pricing and cancellation.
- Real pilot outcomes without unsupported grade claims.
