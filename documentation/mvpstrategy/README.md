# Swedish Curriculum-Aligned Learning Programme — MVP Strategy

## Purpose

This folder defines the validation-first MVP strategy for AsimovX Technologies AB's independently developed AI learning platform.

The product is **not** positioned as another general AI tutor, homework-answering chatbot, PDF chat tool, or Biology application. The proposed commercial wedge is:

> A parent-managed, curriculum-aware test-preparation programme that uses a child's actual Swedish school material, diagnoses learning gaps, manages daily preparation, measures improvement, and keeps the parent informed.

Biology for Swedish school years 7–9 is the initial pilot subject because it fits the existing document, retrieval, knowledge-graph, quiz, notes, study-plan, and progress capabilities without first requiring the symbolic verification needed for Mathematics.

## Current strategic conclusion

- The underlying technical capabilities are useful, but most visible AI-study features are already commoditised.
- Alice, Studi/Binogi, general-purpose AI products, and international K–12 tutors cover much of the feature set.
- Lgr22 alignment, multilingual explanations, or parent reporting alone are not sufficient differentiation.
- The unproven opportunity is the complete Swedish parent workflow: actual school material, upcoming-test goal, diagnosis, managed preparation, improvement evidence, and parent visibility.
- Further investment must be earned through a small real-parent pilot rather than justified by adding more features.

## Governing principles

1. **Validate before expanding.** No additional subject, country, school portal, or native application until the first paid pilot passes its gates.
2. **Teach rather than answer.** The system should require student effort, provide progressive help, and check independent understanding.
3. **Measure learning, not activity.** Sessions, messages, and time spent are supporting metrics; demonstrated improvement is the outcome.
4. **Use AI as infrastructure.** Foundation models may be changed or combined. Curriculum workflow, mastery evidence, parent trust, safety, and distribution are the product.
5. **Use the smallest credible curriculum scope.** Biology is a pilot vehicle, not the permanent identity of the company.
6. **Design for children from the beginning.** Parent control, privacy, safety, data minimisation, and transparent AI use are MVP requirements.
7. **Stop when evidence is weak.** A failed pilot should trigger correction, repositioning, or termination—not automatic feature expansion.

## MVP success definition

The MVP is successful only if it demonstrates all three outcomes:

1. Students repeatedly follow the preparation programme.
2. Controlled pre/post assessment shows meaningful improvement.
3. Parents understand the value and pay to use it again.
